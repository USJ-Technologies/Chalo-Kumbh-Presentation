Real-Time Issue Reporting

Citizen-Powered Environmental Monitoring

Any citizen can report an environmental issue — garbage, pollution, or unclean areas — pinpointed on a live map of Haridwar district.

4 Issue Categories
Garbage · Pollution · Dirty Area · Other — each colour-coded on the map
Location Picker
Citizen selects exact location on an interactive Leaflet map — geo-tagged and publicly visible
Photo Upload
Photographic evidence attached to each report for transparency and faster resolution
Status Tracking
Reports tracked from Filed → In Progress → Resolved, visible to all citizens
Government benefit: Real-time visibility into cleanliness issues across the Mela grounds — enabling faster deployment of sanitation teams.
